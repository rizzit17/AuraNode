Meta Platforms (formerly Facebook) has focused heavily on open-source AI and computer vision acquisitions. In 2013, Meta acquired MobileEye technology assets and hired AI pioneer Yann LeCun to lead FAIR (Fundamental AI Research). Meta later acquired Scruffy AI in 2020 and Mapillary in 2020 to bolster its spatial computing and computer vision research. Meta released the open-source Llama model family, including Llama 2 and Llama 3, positioning itself as a leader in open foundational models.

Nvidia Corporation has strengthened its AI hardware and software software ecosystem through major acquisitions. In March 2020, Nvidia completed its acquisition of Mellanox Technologies for $6.9 billion, integrating high-performance networking data center infrastructure with Nvidia GPU clusters. Nvidia also attempted to acquire ARM Holdings from SoftBank for $40 billion in 2020, though the transaction was ultimately terminated due to regulatory hurdles in 2022. In 2024, Nvidia acquired Run:ai to optimize GPU compute workload orchestration for enterprise AI development.

Apple Inc. has quietly acquired over 30 AI startups between 2018 and 2024, more than any other major tech company. Notable acquisitions include Xnor.ai in 2020 for $200 million, which specialized in low-power edge AI execution, Voicery in 2020 for synthetic speech generation, and WaveOne in 2023 for AI-powered video compression. Apple integrates these technologies directly into Apple Intelligence and local neural engines on iPhone and Mac devices.
