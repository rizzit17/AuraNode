Microsoft Corporation has made significant strategic investments and acquisitions in the artificial intelligence sector. In January 2023, Microsoft announced a multi-billion dollar investment in OpenAI, the creator of ChatGPT and GPT-4. Under this partnership, Microsoft provides Azure cloud computing infrastructure to power OpenAI's workload while integrating OpenAI models into Microsoft products, including Bing Chat and Microsoft 365 Copilot. Prior to OpenAI, Microsoft acquired Nuance Communications in March 2022 for $19.7 billion to expand its healthcare AI capabilities. Nuance specializes in conversational AI and ambient intelligence for healthcare providers.

Google (Alphabet Inc.) has also aggressively expanded its AI capabilities through key acquisitions. In 2014, Google acquired DeepMind, a London-based artificial intelligence research laboratory, for approximately $500 million. DeepMind gained global acclaim for developing AlphaGo, which defeated world champion Go player Lee Sedol in 2016, and AlphaFold, which revolutionized protein structure prediction. In April 2023, Google merged DeepMind with the Google Brain research team to form Google DeepMind, led by Demis Hassabis as CEO. Additionally, Google acquired Kaggle in 2017, a data science community platform, and Anthropic investment backing in 2023 to compete in foundational LLM research.
